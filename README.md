# 🌐 MeshLAN - Your Private Network, Without The Headaches

## 🚀 What Is MeshLAN?

MeshLAN is a powerful yet simple application that lets you create your own private, secure virtual network. Think of it as a secret tunnel that connects your computers together, no matter where they are in the world. Whether you're at home, in a coffee shop, or traveling, MeshLAN makes your devices feel like they're all in the same room.

This self-hosted, peer-to-peer (P2P) virtual LAN solution is perfect for:
- **Gaming with friends** - Play LAN games as if everyone's on the same Wi-Fi
- **Sharing files** - Access files on your home computer from anywhere
- **Collaborating** - Work on projects with colleagues across the globe
- **Running services** - Host websites, databases, or applications securely

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔗 Peer-to-Peer First
MeshLAN connects your devices directly to each other, not through a central server. This means faster speeds, lower latency, and more privacy. Your data goes straight from point A to point B, encrypted and secure.

### 🌍 Multi-Relay Support
If direct connections aren't possible (due to firewalls or network restrictions), MeshLAN automatically uses relay servers to keep you connected. It's like having a helpful friend pass messages between two people who can't talk directly.

### 🤖 AI Automation
MeshLAN includes smart automation features that learn your usage patterns and optimize your network automatically. It can adjust connections, prioritize traffic, and troubleshoot issues without you lifting a finger.

### 🛡️ Built on Nebula Technology
MeshLAN is powered by Nebula, a battle-tested networking technology used by professionals worldwide. This means you get enterprise-grade security and reliability in a user-friendly package.

### 💻 Works on Windows
Specifically designed to run smoothly on Windows operating systems, MeshLAN integrates seamlessly with your existing setup.
